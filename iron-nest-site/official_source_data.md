核心玩法循环：
1. 电传打字机接收最高司令部命令和前线呼叫
2. 将情报转化为作战计划，获取航空照片确认射击效果
3. 在地图上测量距离
4. 弹道计算器计算射击解
5. 调整装药量和仰角
6. 装填弹药（AP穿甲、烟雾弹、光气弹）
7. 拉动发射杆
8. 评估结果，等待新命令

## 来源 4: Steam 官方 FAQ（steamcommunity.com/app/2950790/discussions/0/604170759802194525/）

### Q: When will it be released?
A: Demo available now. Official release August 6, 2026. Full 1.0 release, no Early Access.

### Q: What platforms?
A: PC first, all major console ports planned for a few months after PC release. VR version also planned. Priority: PC → consoles → VR.

### Q: Will IRON NEST have multiplayer?
A: Heavy Turret Simulator launches as fully single-player. Due to demand, will receive multiplayer mode later (each player commands their own IRON NEST on the same map, coop + competitive modes). Separate standalone co-op game planned for late 2027 or early 2028.

### Q: Final price?
A: $15.99 USD including 20% launch discount. Standard price $19.99 USD. Regionally localized.

### Q: Will the cat be separate DLC?
A: No, cat companion is part of base game. Originally planned as DLC but decided not to lock gameplay content behind paywall.

### Q: What does "simulator" mean?
A: Nothing is automated. No shortcuts, no hand-holding, no fake systems. Every lever, valve, and mechanism has purpose. Reloading means physically pulling each handle, engaging loading bridge, locking chamber, feeding each powder charge manually.
